About Me

"Technology gives us power.
I want to use it to create meaning."

Central to my work are the themes of journey, choice, and direction. I don't just write code; I build vessels for meaning—making the invisible internal world visible through digital interaction.

From Text to Tech

Transitioning from Literature to Programming revealed a truth: code is a language of construction. It transforms abstract stories into 3D experiences.

Philosophy

I approach games not as mere entertainment, but as cultural landscapes.I focus on how users generate emotional feedback and make behavioral decisions during the interaction process, and I try to enhance the immersion and long-term experience value through AI-driven system design.

Goal

To create digital spaces that feel human—offering connection, warmth, and a moment of recognition.
